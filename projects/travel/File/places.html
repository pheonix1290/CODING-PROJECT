Best Time to Visit

Kenya can be visited throughout much of the year, but different seasons offer different experiences

☀️ January – March — Hot & Mostly Dry

Weather: Warm to hot, generally dry, with
temperatures varying considerably by location.
Coastal areas tend to be warmer and more humid,
while higher areas can be cooler.

    What you can experiences:

  • Excellent conditions for safari and wildlife viewing

  • Clearer skies for photography

  • Beach holidays along the Kenyan coast

  • Hiking and outdoor activities

Good for:🦁 Safari | 🏖️ Beaches | 📸 Photography | 🥾 Outdoor adventures
🌧️ April – June — Long Rainy Season

Weather:This is Kenya's main long-rain period.
Rain can make some safari roads more difficult to
travel, particularly in parks and reserves.

What you can experience:

  • Green, vibrant landscapes

  • Waterfalls and fuller rivers

  • Fewer tourists in some destinations

  • Beautiful scenery for photography

  • Excellent opportunities for birdwatching

Good for:🌿 Landscapes | 🐦 Birdwatching | 📸 Photography | 💰 Quieter travel
🌦️ November – December — Short Rains & Green Landscapes

Weather:Kenya experiences another, generally
shorter rainy period around November and into December.
The rain can transform previously dry landscapes into
greener environments.

What u can experience:

  • Fresh green scenery

  • Wildlife enjoying newly available vegetation

  • Excellent birdwatching opportunities

  • Fewer visitors than during some peak periods

  • Christmas and holiday travel along the coast

Fun Fact

November can also be particularly interesting for birdwatchers
because migratory birds pass through Kenya around the rainy seasons

Good for🐦 Birdwatching | 🌿 Nature | 📸 Photography | 🏖️ Coastal holidays
